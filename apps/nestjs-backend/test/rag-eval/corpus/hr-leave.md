# Leave policy

## Annual leave

Full-time employees have 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, plus public holidays. Part-time employees get a pro-rata share. Leave is requested in the HR system at least two weeks ahead for periods longer than three days.

## Carrying leave over

Up to 5 unused days can be carried into the next year. Carried-over days must be taken before 31 March; anything left after that date lapses. Managers can approve an exception in writing when a project made it impossible to take the leave.

## Sick leave

Report sickness to your manager before 09:00 on the first day. A doctor's note is required from the fourth consecutive day of absence. Sick days do not reduce annual leave.

## Parental leave

Parents receive 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave, which can be split into up to three blocks within the first year after the birth or adoption. Tell HR at least eight weeks before the first block starts.
