# Pricing and discounts

## List prices

Starter costs 49 EUR per user per month, Business costs 99 EUR per user per month, and Enterprise is priced per contract. Prices exclude VAT. The minimum Business contract is five seats.

## Discounts

Account executives may give up to 15 percent discount on their own. Anything above 15 percent needs approval from the sales director, and above 30 percent from the CEO. Discounts are never given on Starter.

## Annual prepayment

Customers who pay a year in advance get two months free. Annual contracts renew automatically unless cancelled 60 days before the renewal date.
