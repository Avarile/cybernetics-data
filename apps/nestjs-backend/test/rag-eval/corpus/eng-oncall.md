# On-call handbook

## Rotation

On-call shifts rotate weekly and hand over on Monday at 09:00 local time. The schedule lives in the paging tool; swap shifts there, not in chat. Each shift has a primary and a secondary engineer.

## Severity levels

SEV1 means customers cannot use the product at all; acknowledge the page within 5 minutes. SEV2 is a major feature broken for many customers; acknowledge within 15 minutes. SEV3 is degraded but usable and is handled during working hours.

## Escalation

If a SEV1 is not mitigated within 30 minutes, escalate to the CTO by phone. Customer communication is posted on the status page by the support lead every 30 minutes during an incident.

## After an incident

Write a blameless postmortem within 5 business days. It covers the timeline, the root cause, what went well and the follow-up actions with owners.
