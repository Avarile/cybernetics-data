# Office guide Copenhagen

## Getting in

The office is on the third floor at Vesterbrogade 12. The door code changes on the first of every month; reception sends the new code by email. Visitors register at reception and wear a badge.

## Meeting rooms

Book rooms as calendar resources. The large room is called Ørsted and seats twelve; the small rooms Bohr and Brahe seat four. Release a booking you no longer need so others can use the room.

## Lunch

Lunch is served at 12:00 in the canteen. The lunch scheme costs 40 DKK per working day and is deducted from salary. Sign up or out in the HR system by the 20th for the next month.
