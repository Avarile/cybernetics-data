# Data retention policy

## Customer data

Customer data is deleted 90 days after a contract ends, unless the customer asks for an earlier deletion. The deletion is confirmed to the customer in writing.

## Backups and logs

Database backups are kept for 35 days. Application logs are kept for 14 days and audit logs for one year. Backups are encrypted and stored in a second region.

## Data subject requests

Requests under the GDPR, such as access or erasure, are answered within 30 days. They are handled by the data protection officer, who can be reached at dpo@.
