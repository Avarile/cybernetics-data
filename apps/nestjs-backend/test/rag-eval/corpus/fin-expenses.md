# Expense policy

## Travel

Book economy class for flights shorter than six hours; premium economy is allowed for longer flights. Trains are preferred for trips under four hours. Hotels should be within the city rate cap of 1,400 DKK per night in Copenhagen and 180 EUR elsewhere in Europe.

## Per diem

The daily allowance for meals on business trips is 550 DKK in Denmark and follows the official state rates abroad. Meals paid by a customer or included in a conference ticket are deducted from the allowance.

## Receipts

Upload every receipt in the expense app within 30 days of the purchase. Receipts older than 90 days are not reimbursed. Expenses are paid out with the next monthly payroll run after approval.

## Mileage

Business driving in a private car is reimbursed at 3.79 DKK per kilometre for the first 20,000 km per year. Log the trip with start, destination and purpose.
