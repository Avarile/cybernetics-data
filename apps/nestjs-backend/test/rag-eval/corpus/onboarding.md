# New hire onboarding

## Before the first day

The hiring manager opens an IT ticket of type ONB at least five working days before the start date. IT creates the accounts and prepares the laptop.

## First day

Pick up the laptop at the IT desk at 09:30. Your buddy shows you the office and joins you for lunch. Set up the password manager and two-factor authentication before anything else.

## The first three months

Agree a 30-60-90 day plan with your manager in the first week. The probation review happens after three months.
